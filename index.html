About

A student who likes making useful little things.

N M Abu Sayed Sarker is a student from Dhaka who enjoys making small web tools in his free time. Programming is just a hobby for him — he usually starts when he runs into a small, annoying problem and wants to see if he can fix it himself.

Away from the computer, he helps manage online communities, works with student groups, and sometimes speaks to students about study habits and staying motivated. Every now and then he also writes poems and short stories.

Easy OMR Scanner

OMR answer sheet checker

A tool for teachers that checks OMR answer sheets with AI. Set up the exam and answer key, scan each sheet with the camera or pick it from the gallery or a PDF, review what the AI read, then get the results as Excel or PDF.
